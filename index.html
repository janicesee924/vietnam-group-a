Passport & Travel Documents

Passport

Your passport must be valid for at least six months from entry into Vietnam and have at least two blank pages. Your booking name must match your passport.

Visa

Singapore passport holders may generally visit visa-free for up to 30 days. Other passport holders should check their own visa requirements and obtain any required visa before departure.

Copies & permits

Keep copies of your passport, visa if required, flight details and insurance policy separately from the originals. Singapore Permanent Residents should check their Re-Entry Permit validity.

  • November falls within the rainy season in Central Vietnam. Da Nang and Hue may have heavy showers, cooler evenings and wet or slippery paths.
  • Bring a light jacket, umbrella or raincoat, and comfortable walking shoes with good grip. Keep a change of clothes and a waterproof cover for your phone in your day bag.

Actual temperatures may vary. The guide may adjust the programme for weather and road conditions. Check the local forecast closer to departure.

Money, Health & Group Travel

💵 Money and mobile data

  • Carry some VND in small notes for small shops, markets and personal purchases. Check the number of zeros on banknotes and confirm prices before paying.
  • Arrange roaming, an eSIM or a local SIM if needed. Check activation and data settings before departure, and save important contacts offline.

🍜 Meals and personal wellbeing

  • Drink sealed bottled or properly treated water, and choose thoroughly cooked food. Carry your usual medicines in your cabin bag, with prescriptions or supporting documents where needed.
  • Tell WTS Travel about food allergies and dietary requirements in advance. Meals prepared without pork or lard are not necessarily halal-certified; confirm any special requirements before travel.
  • Inform your tour leader promptly if you feel unwell or need assistance. Keep valuables close in crowded places and take care when crossing roads.
Travelling with the group: Follow the daily meeting times and tell the guide before leaving the group. Wear suitable clothing at places of worship.

Baggage & Final Reminders

Baggage allowance

  • Checked baggage: 25 kg per person. Families or groups travelling together may combine their baggage allowances.
  • Cabin baggage (SQ Economy): one piece up to 7 kg. The combined length, width and height must not exceed 115 cm.
  • Cabin liquids, aerosols and gels must be in containers of 100 ml or less, inside one transparent resealable bag with a maximum capacity of 1 litre. Pack permitted sharp items in checked baggage.

🔋 Power banks on Singapore Airlines

≤ 100 WhNo approval needed

  • Maximum of two power banks per passenger. Carry them in your cabin baggage, with the capacity clearly marked, and protect them against short circuits.
  • Do not put power banks in checked baggage or overhead compartments. Keep them accessible, such as in your seat pocket or under the seat in front.
  • Do not charge a power bank or use it to charge other devices during the flight. Follow all instructions from the cabin crew.
Returning to Singapore: Submit your SG Arrival Card and electronic health declaration from 26 to 28 November 2026, before arriving in Singapore on 28 November. This also applies to Singapore residents returning by air. Submission through ICA is free.
